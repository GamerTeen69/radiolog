Log every song played on your PC with the date and time it was heard. Made for internet radio, and it works with any player.

## New: clear your history

`radiolog clear` deletes songs from your history: all of them, chosen songs, or a time range.

```
radiolog clear                     # delete the whole history
radiolog clear 12                  # delete song #12
radiolog clear 3 5 8-12            # delete several songs
radiolog clear --today             # delete today's songs
radiolog clear --until 30d         # delete songs older than 30 days
radiolog clear --search "ad break" # delete matching entries (adverts, jingles)
```

- It shows what will be deleted and asks you to type **yes** (`-y` skips the question).
- It saves a **backup** of your history first (`radiolog-backup-<date>.db`, next to the database), so a mistake can be undone. `--no-backup` skips it.
- Downloaded songs and settings are never touched.

## Install or update

1. Download **radiolog-1.6.0.zip** below and unzip it (over the old folder if you're updating).
2. **Windows:** double-click `install.bat`. **Linux / macOS:** run `./install.sh`.
3. Open a **new** terminal. Your history, downloads and settings are kept.

New to radiolog? The first start asks where to keep your song history and downloads. See the [README](https://github.com/GamerTeen69/radiolog#readme) for the full guide.
