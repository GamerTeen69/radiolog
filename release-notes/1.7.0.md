Log every song played on your PC with the date and time it was heard. Made for internet radio, and it works with any player.

## New: sort by artist, title and genre

```
radiolog show --sort artist            # A-Z by artist ("The Hives" sorts under H)
radiolog show --sort artist --unique   # each song once, with how often it was played (×3)
radiolog show --sort genre             # grouped under genre headings
radiolog show --genre rock --today     # only today's rock songs
```

**Genres** are looked up in free music databases (Deezer, then iTunes; no account needed) the first time they're needed, and remembered, so after the first run it's instant.

## New: sorted downloads

**Choose by genre, in your order:**
```
radiolog download --genre rock                 # every rock song in your history
radiolog download --today --sort artist        # today's songs, A-Z by artist
radiolog pick --genre punk --sort artist       # pick from a sorted, filtered list
```

**Sort the files into folders:** `radiolog config --organize LAYOUT` (or `--organize` for one download)

| Layout | Result |
|---|---|
| `flat` (default) | everything in one folder |
| `artist` | `Green Day\Green Day - Haushinka.opus` |
| `genre` | `Rock\Green Day - Haushinka.opus` |
| `genre-artist` | `Rock\Green Day\Green Day - Haushinka.opus` |

Changing the layout doesn't move songs you already have, and radiolog still recognizes them in any subfolder, so nothing is downloaded twice.

**Proper tags:** every downloaded song now gets radiolog's artist, title and genre written into the file (YouTube's "artist" is usually just the channel name), so your music player can sort them too. Cover art is kept.

Also: `radiolog clear --genre …` removes songs of a genre from your history.

## Install or update
Already on 1.6.1 or newer: `radiolog update`. Otherwise download **radiolog-1.7.0.zip** below, unzip it and run `install.bat` (Windows) or `./install.sh`. Your history, downloads and settings are kept.
