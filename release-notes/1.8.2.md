## New: one playlist with everything, like Shazam's

```
radiolog playlist
```

With nothing else, **all** your songs go into one Spotify playlist called **radiolog**. Run it again whenever you like: only the songs heard since are added, with no duplicates.

- `radiolog playlist --by genre` / `--by artist` without a selection now also cover your whole history (before: the last 50 songs).
- `radiolog playlist --all` still works as before, as *radiolog · All songs*.

First time using Spotify playlists? It needs Spotify Premium and a one-time setup: `radiolog playlist --setup`, or see the [README](https://github.com/GamerTeen69/radiolog#one-time-setup-needs-spotify-premium).

## Install or update
Already on 1.6.1 or newer: `radiolog update`. Otherwise download **radiolog-1.8.2.zip** below, unzip it and run `install.bat` (Windows) or `./install.sh`. Your history, downloads and settings are kept.
