**Test release for sorting by artist, title and genre.** Please report anything that looks wrong.

## New: sort your history

```
radiolog show --sort artist            # A-Z by artist ("The Hives" sorts under H)
radiolog show --sort artist --unique   # each song once, with how often it was played (×3)
radiolog show --sort title             # A-Z by title
radiolog show --sort genre             # grouped under genre headings
radiolog show --genre rock --today     # only today's rock songs
radiolog show --with-genre             # genre in [brackets] next to each song
```

- **Genres** are looked up in free music databases (Deezer, then iTunes; no account needed) the first time they're needed, and remembered. The first `--sort genre` over a long history takes a moment (progress is shown, Ctrl+C skips the rest); after that it's instant.
- Songs no database knows are listed under *Unknown genre* and checked again after 30 days.
- Sorted views cover your whole history unless you add `--today`, `--since` or `-n`.
- `--csv` exports get *genre* and *plays* columns.
