## New: `--all`, for every song in your history

```
radiolog playlist --all            # one Spotify playlist with everything ("radiolog · All songs")
radiolog download --all            # download your whole history (asks first)
radiolog show --all                # the whole list instead of the last 50
radiolog pick --all                # choose from everything instead of the last 20
radiolog clear --all               # same as plain "clear"
```

- Each song is counted once, even if the radio played it many times.
- Combines with other options: `--all --genre rock` gives all your rock songs, and `--all -n 100` gives the newest 100.
- Running `radiolog playlist --all` again later adds only the new songs to the same playlist.

## Install or update
Already on 1.6.1 or newer: `radiolog update`. Otherwise download **radiolog-1.8.1.zip** below, unzip it and run `install.bat` (Windows) or `./install.sh`. Your history, downloads and settings are kept.
