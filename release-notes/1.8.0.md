Log every song played on your PC with the date and time it was heard. Made for internet radio, and it works with any player.

## New: Spotify playlists

Turn your history into playlists in your Spotify account:

```
radiolog playlist --today                          # today's songs
radiolog playlist 3 5 8-12 --name "Road trip"      # chosen songs
radiolog playlist --genre rock --name "Radio rock" # all rock songs
radiolog playlist --by genre                       # one playlist per genre
radiolog playlist --by artist --min 3              # one per artist with 3+ different songs
```

In `radiolog pick`, select songs and press **[p]**.

- **Running it again updates the playlist:** only new songs are added, with no duplicates (`--new` for a separate one).
- Songs Spotify doesn't have are listed at the end. Playlists are private unless you add `--public`.

**One-time setup (needs Spotify Premium):** Spotify only lets small apps create playlists through your *own* free Spotify developer app. Run `radiolog playlist --setup` for the steps, or see [the README](https://github.com/GamerTeen69/radiolog#one-time-setup-needs-spotify-premium). In short:
1. https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard → **Create app**, with Redirect URI `http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback` and **Web API** ticked
2. Copy the **Client ID** from the app's Settings
3. `radiolog config --spotify-client-id YOUR_CLIENT_ID`
4. The first `radiolog playlist` opens Spotify's **Allow** page. Click *Agree*, and radiolog stays connected.

Your password never goes through radiolog, and radiolog contains no one's Client ID.

## New: Spotify searches open in the app

`radiolog search NUMBER --spotify` and **[s]** in `pick` now open in the **Spotify app** when it's installed (including the Microsoft Store version), otherwise in the browser. `--web` uses the browser once; `radiolog config --spotify-open auto|app|web` sets it permanently.

## Install or update
Already on 1.6.1 or newer: `radiolog update`. Otherwise download **radiolog-1.8.0.zip** below, unzip it and run `install.bat` (Windows) or `./install.sh`. Your history, downloads and settings are kept.
